📋 What Changed.ppt - City Slides
FileEditViewInsertFormatSlide ShowHelp
Slide 1 of 3 — Resident Success Client Tracking
Staff-Facing
◀ Before
A time-intensive spreadsheet that got in the way of client work — error-prone, text-heavy, and difficult to navigate unless an appointment was already on the books.
After ▶
A right-sized workbook that supports the team's day-to-day — better-organized components protect data integrity and free up staff for the client work that matters most.
Felt by: Resident Success team
Slide 2 of 3 — Building Permit Applications
Resident-Facing
◀ Before
PDF forms, often brought in person — error-prone, requiring multiple trips and back-and-forth before a permit could move forward.
After ▶
Fully digital submission with automatic routing to building department staff. More accurate. No in-person trip required.
Felt by: Residents, business owners, Building Department staff
Slide 3 of 3 — southbendindiana.gov Accessibility
City-Wide · ADA Title II
◀ Before
50% compliance score. Templates never designed to meet ADA standards. Real barriers for residents using assistive technology.
After ▶
95% compliant. Code-level template changes, page-by-page remediation. A standard now carried across all 20+ city domains.

Compliance progress:
50%
95%
Felt by: All residents, especially those using assistive technology
